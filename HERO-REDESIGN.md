# 🍎 BrandQraft Hero Section — Apple-Like Premium Redesign

## ✨ What's Been Updated

### Visual Design Changes

#### 1. **Color Palette** — Pure Black & White
- ✅ Background: Pure white (#FFFFFF)
- ✅ Text: Pure black (#000000)
- ✅ Subtitle: Dark gray (#3A3A3A)
- ✅ Removed all blue accent colors
- ✅ Added subtle gradient overlay (black to silver tones)

#### 2. **Typography** — Apple System Fonts
- ✅ **Headline**: 80-100px (responsive), Semibold, -0.04em letter-spacing
- ✅ **Subheadline**: 22-24px, Regular weight, #3A3A3A color
- ✅ **Font Stack**: -apple-system, SF Pro Display, Helvetica Neue
- ✅ **Line Height**: Tight 1.05 for headline, 1.5 for body
- ✅ **Alignment**: Left-aligned (not centered) for power and focus

#### 3. **Layout** — Spacious & Minimal
- ✅ Left-aligned content (Apple style)
- ✅ 8% horizontal padding for breathing room
- ✅ Removed colorful gradient orbs
- ✅ Added subtle black-to-silver gradient overlay
- ✅ Clean, uncluttered composition

#### 4. **Buttons** — Refined Apple Style
- ✅ **Primary Button**: 
  - Black fill with white text
  - Pill-shaped (border-radius: 980px)
  - Subtle gradient overlay on hover
  - Scale effect (1.03x) on hover
  - Smooth cubic-bezier easing

- ✅ **Secondary Button**:
  - Black outline, transparent fill
  - Fills with black on hover (text turns white)
  - Same pill shape
  - Smooth transitions

#### 5. **Animations** — Subtle & Refined
- ✅ Slow gradient shift (20s animation)
- ✅ Gentle parallax on scroll (0.3x speed)
- ✅ Fade-in entrance animation
- ✅ No bouncy or flashy animations
- ✅ Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`

#### 6. **Navigation Bar** — Apple-Style Blur
- ✅ Translucent background (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72))
- ✅ Backdrop blur with saturation (like Apple)
- ✅ 0.5px border (ultra-thin)
- ✅ Clean 14px font size for links
- ✅ Opacity hover effect (0.6) instead of color change
- ✅ Pill-shaped CTA button

### Technical Improvements

#### Performance
- ✅ Passive scroll listeners
